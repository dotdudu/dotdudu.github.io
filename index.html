01 — Background

My undergraduate thesis on top management team heterogeneity and corporate digital transformation first pulled me into the question: how do institutions really change? Now working in the economic sector, I see that question play out every day — in fiscal policy, in rural development, in the friction between data and decision.

I keep learning through probability, public finance, and the kind of systems thinking that good games teach you. This space is where I collect what I'm reading, what I'm practicing, and what I'm wondering about.
